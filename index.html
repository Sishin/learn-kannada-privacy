Privacy Policy

Learn Kannada – Fun Learning (Android and iOS) · Effective date: 21 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Learn Kannada app ("the App") handles. The App is made by AgentCodeApps to help young children learn the Kannada alphabet.

1. Who the App is for

The App is designed for children and the parents who teach them. It follows the Google Play Families policy and Apple's rules for apps made for kids.

2. Information we collect

We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no way to enter a name, email address, phone number or photo.

The App keeps your child's learning progress (stars, streaks and traced letters) only on the device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the App deletes this progress.

Letter and word sounds use the device's own text-to-speech voice. The App does not record or use the microphone, camera, contacts or location.

3. Advertising

The App is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Because the App is made for children:

  • every ad request is marked as child-directed and as coming from a user under the age of consent;
  • ads are non-personalised and limited to content rated suitable for general audiences (G);
  • the App does not use the device's advertising ID.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may process limited technical information such as the device's IP address (used for approximate location), device model, operating system and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data. This is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. We do not receive information that identifies your child.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information. Apart from the ad information described above, which goes directly to Google AdMob, the App sends no data off the device.

5. Security

All network traffic from the App, including ad requests, is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

6. Parents' choices

You can stop all ad-related processing by turning off the device's internet connection while your child uses the App; the lessons, tracing and stories work offline. You can delete all progress by uninstalling the App.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

8. Contact
